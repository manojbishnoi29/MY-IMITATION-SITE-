Our story

Fifteen years of shine, one piece at a time

Noor Jewels began as a small counter in Bharatpur with a simple idea: beautiful jewellery shouldn't cost a month's salary. Today we design and finish every piece ourselves, working with karigars who have spent generations perfecting Kundan, Meenakari and antique gold work.

We sell only imitation jewellery — no real gold, no precious stones, no inflated prices. Just honest craft, delivered to your door.
